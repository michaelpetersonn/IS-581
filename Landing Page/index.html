HOW C3NSOR WORKS

Prevent. Understand. Act.

01

Prevent

Block known advertising and cross-site trackers.

02

Understand

Find and summarize the website’s privacy policy with citations.

03

Act

Help you opt out, request access or deletion, or leave the website.

WHAT IT CAN DO

Before you
give a site data.

The extension can still protect you on the page — without sending your browsing history anywhere.

01

Block trackers first

Stop known third-party trackers before their requests leave the browser.

02

Control cookies

Prevent or remove cookies from selected requests.

03

Use a cached analysis

Retrieve an existing privacy-policy analysis when one is already available.

04

Read unknown policies

Analyze an unknown policy while blocking other site resources.

05

Warn before you agree

Alert you before you log in, accept cookies, or submit information.

06

Processed on your device

The extension works locally. Nothing about where you browse is recorded.
