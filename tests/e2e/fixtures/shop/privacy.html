Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Fixture Shop collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit our website or buy from us.

Information we collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us, such as your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details when you place an order. We also collect device information, your IP address, and browsing activity through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, process orders, personalize your shopping experience, and send you marketing messages about products we think you will like. We also use data for analytics and fraud prevention.

How we share your information

We may share your personal information with service providers who help us run the store, such as payment processors and shipping carriers. We also share data with advertising partners and our affiliates for the purposes described in this policy.

Advertising and the sale of personal information

We use targeted advertising and may share your personal information with advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising. Under California law this may be considered a sale of personal information. You can opt out at any time using our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.

Your choices and rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. To make a request, visit our privacy request page or contact us at privacy@fixture-shop.example. We respond to verified requests within 45 days as required by the CCPA and GDPR.

Data retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, and we protect it with reasonable security measures.
