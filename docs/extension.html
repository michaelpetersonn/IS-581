WHAT IT DOES TODAY

Understand first.
Act with links.

01

Find the policy

Scans the page for privacy links, or lets you paste a policy URL.

02

Five checkpoints

Collection, use, sharing, advertising/sale language, and your choices — each with a citation.

03

Stays local

No cloud analysis. No browsing history product. Session cache only, clear anytime.

04

Honest limits

Rules, not legal advice. Some JS-heavy privacy hubs (like Meta) may need a printable URL.

INSTALL (DEVELOPER MODE)

Load unpacked
in Chrome.

  1. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Load unpacked and select the project’s extension/ folder.
  3. Pin c3nsor, open a normal https site, and click the icon.

Prefer DuckDuckGo, Mozilla, or Wikimedia for demos. Avoid SPA-only privacy centers as your first click.
