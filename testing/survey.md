# Post-session survey

Paste these questions into a Google Form. Tester fills it in right after the session (about 3 minutes, on any device). Every tester answers every question, whether they used only the Try it page or also the extension. In Form settings, turn **off** "Collect email addresses" so responses stay anonymous; the tester ID links the response to the session notes.

Form title: **c3nsor feedback**
Form description: "Thanks for testing c3nsor. Your answers are anonymous and only used to improve the product."
